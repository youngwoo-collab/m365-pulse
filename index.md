# M365 Pulse

M365 Pulse is a desktop and mobile app that checks, from your own device, whether Microsoft 365
services — mail, Teams and SharePoint — are working for you. It tells apart a Microsoft outage
from a problem on your own network, sign-in or device.

## What it does

- Reads Microsoft's published Service Health and shows open incidents.
- Sends small, clearly labeled automated test items (a mail, a Teams post, a file) in your own
  Microsoft 365 account, confirms they arrive, and deletes them.
- Optionally connects a personal Gmail account to check mail sent to and from outside
  Microsoft 365. This uses Google's `gmail.send` and `gmail.modify` scopes, only for the app's own
  test message — see the privacy policy for exactly what is accessed.
- Runs entirely on your device. There is no M365 Pulse server, account system or analytics, and
  nothing is sent to the developer.
